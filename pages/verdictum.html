Product summary

Verdictum is a sanctions-aware compliance and operational intelligence platform designed to help organizations screen entities, validate field information, and generate audit-support evidence.

It focuses on compliance workflows, secure data intake, structured verification, and operational decision support.

The problem

Fragmented screening

Sanctions and counterparty screening workflows are often split across separate tools, spreadsheets, manual checks, and undocumented review steps.

Weak verification records

Teams may know that a check was performed, but lack clean records showing what was checked, when it was checked, and what result was produced.

Operational context gaps

Compliance checks are often disconnected from field data, operational events, logistics context, or real-world verification workflows.

The solution

Verdictum provides a structured platform for sanctions-aware screening, verification workflows, and compliance-oriented operational intelligence.

It helps teams collect, process, review, and document verification results in a cleaner and more explainable way.

How Verdictum helps

Screening workflows

Support screening processes against sanctions-related datasets, watchlist-style sources, and compliance review inputs.

Structured verification

Help organize identity, entity, document, and operational verification steps into a more consistent workflow.

Evidence generation

Produce clearer records of checks, decisions, timestamps, review status, and operational context for audit-support purposes.

Target users

Verdictum is relevant for compliance teams, security operations teams, logistics operators, financial technology teams, critical infrastructure operators, and organizations working with regulated or high-risk counterparties.
