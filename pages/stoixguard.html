Field Operations

stoiXGuard

Operational Intelligence for Rapidly Deployed Field Operations

ADVANCED PRE-PRODUCTION

Architecture and core product capability are mature; final production hardening and acceptance are still in progress.

stoiXGuard creates a unified operational environment for people, mobile devices, communications, maps, incidents, evidence, and the Operational Plan.

A defining property of Guard is the quality of the operational picture itself. Expected, observed, and inferred state remain separate; OverWatch analyzes discrepancies; the Operational Decision Assistant supports the next decision without acquiring command authority.

Operational reality

Field Reality Continuously Changes

Guard qualifies divergence instead of silently averaging it away.

Stale Coordinates

Old location data remains explicitly stale rather than becoming current by assumption.

Degraded Connectivity

Loss of signal is distinguished from loss of the person, device, or mission state.

Missing Devices

Missing, offline, and unreachable remain different operational conditions.

Conflicting Reports

Human reports and telemetry may disagree; conflict remains visible until qualified.

Deployment

Rapid Deployment

With prepared endpoints and connectivity, a working FieldSession can be established without building heavy local infrastructure first.

For a small group of approximately five people using prepared phones, a practical target is roughly 7–15 minutes, depending on device readiness, connectivity, and the deployment profile.

State discipline

Expected, Observed, and Inferred State Stay Separate

EXPECTED ≠ OBSERVED
OBSERVED ≠ INFERRED
MISSING ≠ VIOLATION
STALE ≠ ABSENT
CONFLICTING ≠ AVERAGED

The Operational Plan defines expected state. People and devices create observations. Analytics creates inference. These levels remain separate.

Supervision

OverWatch

OverWatch compares the Operational Plan, observations, freshness, and current operational state.

It identifies meaningful discrepancies and helps determine which additional observation has the highest value.

Decision support

Operational Decision Assistant

The assistant combines FieldSession, incidents, device state, connectivity, Operational Memory, and verified outcomes.

Human witness

Operator Witness

A human operator connects directly to the bounded context of the situation, while voice and media become part of the wider operational workflow.

Distributed presence

STOIX Node

Selected capabilities can be placed closer to the field environment: local evidence, communications, bounded state, semantic processing, human interaction, and qualified policy enforcement.

Placement ≠ Authority

Safety boundary

SOS Remains Deterministic

SOS is a deterministic operational path. Analytical assistants and OverWatch can provide context, but they do not gate or delay SOS initiation.

Threat verdicts remain human decisions; optional face detection does not become autonomous threat authority.

Operational Memory

Material events, evidence, decisions, communication references, actions, and verified outcomes are retained so the operation can be reconstructed after the FieldSession.
