stoiXGuard

Operational Intelligence for Rapidly Deployed Field Operations

ADVANCED PRE-PRODUCTION

Architecture and core product capability are mature; final production hardening and acceptance are still in progress.

stoiXGuard creates a unified operational environment for people, mobile devices, communications, maps, incidents, evidence, and the Operational Plan. The system is designed for situations in which field reality changes faster than traditional command infrastructure can reconstruct it: connectivity degrades, coordinates become stale, devices disappear from the network, and human reports may conflict with telemetry.

A defining property of Guard is the quality of the operational picture itself. It explicitly separates expected, observed, and inferred state, uses OverWatch to analyze discrepancies, and applies the Operational Copilot to support the next decision without turning analytical recommendations into hidden command authority.

Shared Telephony, Operator Witness, evidence-aware communications, and STOIX Node connect people, communications, and local operational context within one workflow. The operator receives more than a map and message stream: Guard provides a qualified view of the situation with explicit freshness, uncertainty, and available evidence.

Field Reality Continuously Changes

Plan, physical reality, and available observations diverge.

Coordinates become stale.

Connectivity degrades.

Devices disappear from the network.

Human reports conflict with telemetry.

Guard qualifies these differences instead of silently averaging them away.

Rapid Deployment

With prepared endpoints and connectivity, a working FieldSession can be established within minutes.

The target deployment scenario is approximately 3–10 minutes, depending on the deployment profile and environmental conditions.

EXPECTED ≠ OBSERVED ≠ INFERRED

MISSING ≠ VIOLATION

STALE ≠ ABSENT

CONFLICTING ≠ AVERAGED

The Operational Plan defines expected state.

People and devices create observations.

Analytics creates inference.

These levels remain separate.

OverWatch

OverWatch compares the plan, observations, freshness, and operational state.

It identifies meaningful discrepancies and helps determine which additional observation has the highest value.

Operational Copilot

OverWatch and the Operational Decision Assistant combine FieldSession, incidents, device state, connectivity, Operational Memory, and verified outcomes.

The Copilot can:

  • explain the situation;
  • identify evidence gaps;
  • compare options;
  • propose the next step.

The distinction remains explicit:

FACT → OBSERVATION → INFERENCE → RECOMMENDATION → AUTHORIZED ACTION → VERIFIED OUTCOME

Communications Inside Context

Guard uses the shared STOIX communications substrate:

Trust + Endpoint Registry + Frontier + Canonical Telephony

Communication is linked to a specific participant, FieldSession, incident, and current operational context.

Operator Witness

A human operator connects directly to the bounded context of the situation, while the voice/media session becomes part of the wider operational workflow.

STOIX Node

Node allows selected capabilities to be placed closer to the field environment:

  • local evidence;
  • communications;
  • bounded state;
  • semantic processing;
  • human interaction;
  • qualified policy enforcement.

Operational Memory

After a FieldSession, material events, evidence, decisions, communication references, actions, and verified outcomes are retained.

This makes it possible to reconstruct what was known, why a decision was made, and how the situation ultimately resolved.
