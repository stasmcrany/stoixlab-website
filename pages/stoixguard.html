Project overview

stoiXGuard is not positioned as a replacement for stoiXDome and is not merely a sanctions-search utility. It is a separate STOIXLAB project direction for civilian event operations and evidence-memory workflows.

The concept is built around teams that arrive at an event, temporary site, controlled area, inspection scenario, or operational checkpoint and need to establish a structured local operational environment quickly.

stoiXGuard can be described as an event-memory and field-verification system: it records what was checked, what was observed, who acted, which media or evidence was attached, what incidents occurred, and what should be reviewed after the operation.

AWS serverless-native deployment option

stoiXGuard can use AWS serverless-native deployment patterns for backend services, synchronization, evidence storage, reporting workflows, and operational audit trails. This can include managed APIs, event-driven compute, managed storage, queues, notifications, and object storage where those services fit the deployment model.

stoiXGuard is not an AWS-only product. The platform concept remains focused on rapid field deployment, local operational workflows, event-specific security needs, and reviewable operational evidence.

Project boundary

Separate from stoiXDome

stoiXGuard remains a separate STOIXLAB project direction. It may share broader trust, evidence, and protected-data ideas with the STOIXLAB ecosystem, but it is not the same product as stoiXDome.

Use-case direction

stoiXGuard is relevant for scenarios where operational teams need temporary, structured event-operations support: events, inspections, site-access coordination points, venue operations, business-continuity contexts, logistics checkpoints, diplomatic or business gatherings, and other environments where operational records must become reviewable evidence.

Approved reference-list inputs may be one customer-authorized input, but they do not define the whole project. The broader purpose is event-operations context, local operational memory, human review, and evidence generation.

Public-safety and control boundary

stoiXGuard is framed for civilian event, venue, inspection, and business-continuity contexts. It does not replace legal, law-enforcement, or identity-authority decisions, and it does not make autonomous identity determinations.

The project direction is built around customer-authorized inputs, customer-controlled retention, role-based access, human review, incident logging, and post-event reporting.

Simple workflow

Create an event workspace, add authorized operators and event context, record incidents, notes, media references, and operator actions, use approved reference-list inputs where customer policy allows, and export a post-event report according to retention policy.

Current maturity

stoiXGuard is currently treated as a significant STOIXLAB project direction under technical review, frontend refinement, testing analysis, and product-boundary clarification.

Public positioning should avoid presenting stoiXGuard as a completed commercial product. It should be presented as a serious civilian event-operations and operational-evidence project with important product potential and clear separation from the completed stoiXDome platform.
