Research & Horizons

New Operational Worlds Built on the Same STOIX Foundation

Research & Horizons brings together two related parts of STOIXLAB: applied directions that can become future systems, and foundational research that extends the capabilities beneath them.

The three current horizons — Electronic Operator, stoiXSea, and Veritas — address very different environments, but share the same discipline around state, evidence, uncertainty, authority, and verified outcomes.

Data Center / Electronic Operator

A modern data center is simultaneously a digital platform, an engineering plant, a physical environment, and a human-operated system. Electronic Operator explores a supervisory layer that reconstructs shared operational state across these domains without replacing PLCs, BMS, hard real-time controllers, or engineering authority.

Cross-Domain State

Compute, network, power, cooling, equipment telemetry, and physical conditions are interpreted as one connected environment.

Temporal Reasoning

Digital and physical changes are reconstructed through time so current state can be separated from the path that produced it.

Governed Intervention

Candidate actions are evaluated against dependencies, constraints, authority, and safety boundaries before intervention.

Verified Outcome

The system checks the resulting physical and operational state after intervention instead of treating command execution as success.

stoiXSea

stoiXSea extends STOIX into maritime and logistics environments where vessel state, machinery, cargo, route, communications, weather, logistics handoffs, and fleet-level dependencies form one evolving physical system.

On-Board Operational State

Machinery, energy, navigation, stabilization, vessel systems, and local telemetry contribute to a shared operational context.

Environment & Cargo

Weather, cargo condition, route, checkpoints, custody, and transfer events remain part of the same evidence model.

Distributed Operation

Intermittent connectivity, local state continuity, delayed synchronization, and bounded local behavior are first-class conditions.

Fleet & Logistics Topology

Vessel, fleet, shipment, handoff, and flow relationships can be analyzed across multiple operational scales.

stoiXSea — Direction

engine sensors → local operational cell → vessel node → fleet

The Digital Twin becomes an operational state model connecting physical state, relationships, constraints, historical context, and candidate interventions.

Placement ≠ Authority

stoiXSea remains a longer-horizon STOIXLAB direction and a potential distributed cyber-physical STOIX system for maritime and logistics environments.

Veritas

Veritas applies the STOIX evidence model to professional and organizational information: who a person or organization claims to be, what relationships existed, when they existed, which sources support those claims, and where evidence conflicts or remains incomplete.

Claim Structure

Statements about people, roles, history, credentials, and organizations remain explicit claims until supported by qualified evidence.

Relationship & Time

People, organizations, projects, roles, and events are linked through temporal and relational context rather than isolated records.

Evidence Conflict

Confirmation, contradiction, source dependence, and unresolved gaps remain visible in the model.

Investigation Path

The system can show which missing source, relationship, or event would have the highest value for further investigation.

Veritas — Direction

Claim → Source → Evidence → Relationship → Time → Confirmation / Conflict / Unknown

Potential applications include professional background verification, organizational relationships, HR risk, procurement analysis, and conflict-of-interest investigation.

Veritas explores evidence-grounded professional and organizational intelligence without collapsing the result into a trust score or automatic fraud verdict.

Foundational Research

Evidence-Governed Expert Fabric

Human experts, LLMs, and specialized solvers working together while preserving provenance, disagreement, uncertainty, and responsibility.

STOIX Node

A universal runtime receiving different operational roles through Environment, Capability, Decision, and Policy profiles. Placement ≠ Authority.

Active Verification

Selecting an observation or bounded diagnostic probe that best distinguishes competing hypotheses and reduces uncertainty. Probe Proposal ≠ Evidence.

Investigation Memory

Using prior cases to accelerate investigation without turning similarity into proof or causality.

IoT / OT & Physical Systems

A supervisory layer above physical systems while hard real-time controllers retain their own control loops.
