Matter Engineering for Complex Legal Disputes

ADVANCED PRE-PRODUCTION

Architecture and core product capability are mature; final production hardening and acceptance are still in progress.

stoiXLaw transforms raw legal material — party claims, contracts, correspondence, payments, events, documents, and legal sources — into a structured Matter in which factual assertions, evidence, chronology, applicable rules, and uncertainty remain separate elements within one model.

Adaptive Inquiry identifies missing Evidence Objectives; discovered legal sources are separately qualified before use; temporal applicability preserves jurisdiction, effective period, exceptions, and the version of the rule in force.

Mandatory Counter-Position adds an adversarial layer before strategy is finalized, testing the initial position against contradictory evidence and a strong opposing case.

Source qualification

Source Bootstrap

A discovered source is a candidate, not authority.

Temporal law

Temporal Applicability

A legal source is evaluated against jurisdiction, effective period, scope, hierarchy, exceptions, and the specific Matter.

The applicable rule is the rule applicable to the event in its historical context, not merely the newest text available today.

Adversarial review

Mandatory Counter-Position

After the initial assessment, the system separately investigates a strong opposing position: contradictory evidence, alternative interpretation, exceptions, chronology weaknesses, and vulnerable claims.

The purpose is not to manufacture symmetry. It is to force the initial position to survive a serious adversarial test.
