stoiXLaw

Matter Engineering for Complex Legal Disputes

ADVANCED PRE-PRODUCTION

Architecture and core product capability are mature; final production hardening and acceptance are still in progress.

stoiXLaw transforms raw legal material — party claims, contracts, correspondence, payments, events, documents, and legal sources — into a structured Matter in which factual assertions, evidence, chronology, applicable rules, and uncertainty exist as separate elements within one model. This allows a dispute to be analyzed as a connected system of claims, relationships, and evidence rather than as a collection of documents waiting to be summarized.

The system directs further investigation through Adaptive Inquiry, identifies missing Evidence Objectives, and separately qualifies discovered legal sources before they are used in reasoning. Temporal applicability makes it possible to account for jurisdiction, the version of the rule in force, effective period, exceptions, and the specific context of the event.

Mandatory Counter-Position adds a separate adversarial layer: the initial position is tested against a strong opposing case, contradictory evidence, and alternative interpretation. The result is therefore a more mature Matter in which supported elements, weak claims, evidence gaps, and questions requiring legal judgment are visible before strategy is finalized.

Matter Engineering

Structured Matter

Parties, relationships, events, chronology, claims, counterclaims, evidence, contracts, obligations, applicable sources, gaps, and uncertainty.

Claim ≠ Fact

A party's position remains a claim. A document remains an evidence object. Interpretation remains a separate analytical layer.

Adaptive Inquiry

The system formulates Evidence Objectives: which fact, relationship, evidence, or source role is missing.

Source Bootstrap

SOURCE_GAP → DISCOVERY → QUALIFICATION → APPLICABILITY.

Discovery ≠ Qualification ≠ Proposition Validity ≠ Legal Conclusion.

Temporal Applicability

A source is evaluated against jurisdiction, effective period, scope, hierarchy, exceptions, and the specific Matter.

Mandatory Counter-Position

The initial assessment is separately tested against contradicting evidence, alternative interpretation, exceptions, chronology weaknesses, and vulnerable claims.

Lawyer-Ready Handoff

The result is a structured working dossier containing chronology, claims, counterclaims, evidence, obligations, qualified sources, gaps, initial position, counter-position, and unresolved questions.
