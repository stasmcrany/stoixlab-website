Company overview

STOIXLAB is a founder-led, export-oriented software technology initiative focused on cloud-security infrastructure, operational evidence systems, protected signal processing, and future logistics/industrial technology.

The company portfolio includes three product directions: stoiXDome as the primary AWS-first cloud-security and trust-infrastructure platform, stoiXGuard as a rapid-deployment event-security and operational-evidence system, and stoiXSea as a long-term logistics and industrial technology concept.

The business direction is suitable for technology review, partnership discussions, legal and economic-review context, European business-development planning, cloud-security service activity, and future product-access conversations.

AWS startup ecosystem

STOIXLAB is developing its cloud-security and trust-infrastructure direction inside the AWS startup ecosystem. The founder has been accepted into AWS Founders Club, and AWS Activate credits have been issued to support product development, testing, and cloud-native deployment work.

This is not an AWS certification, product validation, or endorsement of STOIXLAB products. It means the company is building with access to AWS startup resources and is actively aligning its cloud architecture with AWS-native services where they are technically appropriate.
