Cloud & Digital Infrastructure

stoiXDome

Cloud Infrastructure Assurance at the Level of Real Topology, State, and Evidence

stoiXDome investigates the cloud environment as a connected operational system in which identities, trust, workloads, permissions, resources, and runtime dependencies are evaluated within a unified context. Instead of stopping at an isolated security finding, Dome reconstructs meaningful relationships, temporal state, and available evidence to show why a given condition represents risk and which parts of the infrastructure are connected to it.

The practical value of this approach becomes especially clear during remediation: the engineer receives not only an indication of the problem, but also dependency context, change constraints, rollback considerations, and a basis for subsequent verification. After the action, Dome re-observes the environment and compares the actual state with the required outcome.

stoiXDome therefore carries an engineering case from an identified cloud risk through investigation and qualified change to an independently verified result, while preserving the provider-native semantics of AWS, Azure, and GCP together with the common STOIX evidence and authority discipline.

From Finding to System Context

An isolated configuration finding rarely shows:

  • which relationships create the risk;
  • what depends on a permission;
  • when the current state emerged;
  • which change is acceptable;
  • which evidence confirms the result.

Dome connects these questions into one investigation and resolution lifecycle.

What Distinguishes stoiXDome

Hidden Trust & Access Paths

Identity, roles, trust, workloads, and resources are evaluated as a topology.

Risk is assessed in the context of actual relationships and dependencies.

Non-Human Identity

Service accounts, workload identities, and CI/CD roles are connected to ownership, privileges, workloads, trust, and evidence.

Temporal Investigation

The cloud environment is investigated through time: configuration changes, trust changes, incidents, and remediation attempts become part of a single state history.

Change Assurance

A candidate change is evaluated together with dependencies, constraints, rollback, freshness, and authority requirements.

Independent Verification

After a change, Dome re-examines the environment and compares the new state with the required outcome.

Multi-Cloud Architecture

AWS, Azure, and GCP retain their provider-native semantics.

Rosetta and Universal Projection create a common analytical layer where meaningful semantic correspondence exists.

Dome as a STOIX Realization

Dome brings together shared STOIX capabilities:

  • evidence acquisition;
  • semantic normalization;
  • canonical and temporal state;
  • relationship analysis;
  • governance;
  • reporting;
  • outcome verification.

This makes stoiXDome the first primary commercial realization of the STOIX architecture.
