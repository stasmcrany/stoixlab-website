Four Properties of stoiXBase

1. State Through Time

A snapshot shows a single moment.

STOIX preserves temporal context: what existed, what changed, what evidence was available, and what happened after an action.

History becomes part of the reasoning model.

2. CAN ≠ MAY

CAN — capability

MAY — authority

The technical ability to perform an operation and the authority to perform it are separate architectural objects.

3. Governed Inaction

If evidence is insufficient, state is stale, authority is missing, or a candidate does not satisfy the requirements, Hold can be a valid governed outcome.

4. Independent Outcome Verification

Execution status confirms that a command was executed.

The actual outcome is confirmed through independent re-observation of the external environment.

Execution ≠ Verified Outcome
