Review tracks

The review can be scoped as one track, two tracks, or a full three-track evidence review. Exact scope depends on AWS environment size, available evidence, and the review goal.

Track 1

IAM / Access Risk

Review IAM users, roles, policies, high-risk permissions, access concentration, and identity-risk findings that should be visible before security or due-diligence review.

Track 2

Evidence & Logging

Review CloudTrail/logging readiness, evidence availability, event visibility, and reportable gaps in AWS activity history.

Track 3

Lambda / Serverless Runtime Safety

Review selected Lambda and serverless runtime evidence, execution boundaries, logging signals, and operational-safety findings.

Read-only boundary

STOIXLAB reviews evidence, prepares findings, and explains practical next steps. The engagement does not make changes to AWS resources and does not replace an internal security owner, legal advisor, or formal compliance decision.

The report is designed for clarity: what was reviewed, what appears risky, what evidence supports the finding, and what should be considered next.
