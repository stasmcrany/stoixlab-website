Investigation — INV001

When the Symptom Is Known but the Root Cause Is Not

INV001 is designed for infrastructure problems with an unknown cause:

  • conflicting telemetry;
  • unexplained privilege changes;
  • runtime anomalies;
  • recurring incidents;
  • failed remediation;
  • hidden dependencies.

Investigation works with competing hypotheses and evidence, preserving unresolved uncertainty where the available data is insufficient.
