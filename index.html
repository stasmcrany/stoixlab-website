What STOIXLAB is building

STOIXLAB is a founder-led, export-oriented software technology initiative focused on cloud-state assessment, graph-vector evidence, protected telemetry movement, temporal state review, operational intelligence, and structured technical reporting.

The public portfolio supports technology review, partnership discussions, legal and economic-review context, European business-development planning, cloud-security service activity, and future product-access conversations.

The architecture direction emphasizes customer-controlled data boundaries, privacy-aware AI-assisted analysis, configurable retention, private evidence delivery, and policy-governed response workflows.

AWS credibility and serverless-native direction

STOIXLAB is building its cloud-security direction with an AWS-first engineering focus. The founder has been accepted into AWS Founders Club, and AWS Activate credits have been issued to support product development, validation, and cloud-native deployment work.

The preferred architecture is serverless-native where it makes operational sense: event-driven compute, managed APIs, managed storage, queue-based workflows, and deployment models that reduce infrastructure overhead while keeping security, auditability, customer control, and controlled failure behavior at the center.
