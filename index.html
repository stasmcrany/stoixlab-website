AWS credibility and serverless-native direction

STOIXLAB is building its cloud-security direction with an AWS-first engineering focus. The founder has been accepted into AWS Founders Club, and AWS Activate credits have been issued to support product development, testing, and cloud-native deployment work.

The preferred architecture is serverless-native where it makes operational sense: event-driven compute, managed APIs, managed storage, queue-based workflows, and deployment models that reduce infrastructure overhead while keeping security, auditability, and controlled failure behavior at the center.
