Architecture for Evidence-Governed Decisions in Complex Reality

STOIXLAB is an engineering and research laboratory building systems for environments where decisions must rely on verifiable state, traceable evidence, explicit authority, and confirmed outcomes.

STOIX connects observation, state, investigation, decision, authority, action, and independent outcome verification within a single architecture.

Why This Layer Matters

Modern operational stacks are split across monitoring, analytics, AI, policy, and automation.

OBSERVE → QUALIFY → RECONSTRUCT → INVESTIGATE → RESOLVE → AUTHORIZE → ACT / HOLD → RE-OBSERVE → VERIFY

This makes it possible to distinguish what is known, how a conclusion was formed, what authority exists, what action occurred, and what evidence confirms the resulting state.
